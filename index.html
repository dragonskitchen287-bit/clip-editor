Auto Clip Editor

Turn a long video into short, ready-to-post clips. The app finds the most energetic moments, crops them for vertical or square, and exports them. Your video never leaves your device.

1. Choose your video

2. Settings

3. Your clips

Adjust start and end times if you like, untick clips you don’t want, then export. Keep this tab open and visible while exporting, because clips are recorded in real time.

4. Post online

Sites only accept posts from your own logged-in account, so finish by picking the exported file there. On a phone, use each clip’s Share button to send it straight to the YouTube, Instagram or TikTok app.

YouTube Shorts TikTok Instagram Facebook Reels

Only upload videos you own or have permission to use. Clips download as MP4 when your browser supports it (recent Chrome, Edge and Safari); otherwise as WebM, which you can convert for sites that need MP4.
